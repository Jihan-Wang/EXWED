Current wording:

Proposed and applied wording:

7. change
Location: LCE construction, Linear-time suffix ordering (original main.tex:728; revised main.tex:727)
Issue: ambiguous referent
Current wording:
Consider two distinct sample positions $p,q$. Their triple
sequences differ before either comparison passes its final triple.
Suppose that a final triple equalled a triple in the other sequence.
Its uniqueness would force the two triples to start at the same
position. The comparisons have advanced by the same number of
triples. Thus they would have started at the same position, contrary
to $p\ne q$. The names preserve the order of the first unequal
triples. The extra $B_2$ names after the $B_1$ sequence in $R$

recursive call preserves the order of the sampled suffixes.
Proposed and applied wording:
For distinct sample positions $p,q$, their triple sequences must differ
by the time either reaches its final triple. Otherwise, uniqueness would
force the two current positions to coincide. Both positions have advanced
by the same multiple of three, so this would imply $p=q$.
Names preserve the order of the first unequal triples. Thus the $B_2$
names appended after the $B_1$ sequence in $R$ cannot change a comparison.
The recursive call therefore preserves the order of the sampled suffixes.
Reason: The two compared samples may belong to the same residue class. Avoid wording that suggests they must come from different residue sequences.

12. change
Location: Proof of prop:integer-gap-distance-sensitive, all-failure branch (original main.tex:1720; revised main.tex:1715)
Issue: routine algebra and complexity ambiguity
Current wording:
Suppose every call fails. Let $L$ be the largest tested threshold.
At least one threshold is tested because $Q\le n^2$.
The next threshold $2L$ is excluded. Thus
\[
 4QL^2>n^2.
\]
Failure at $L$ implies $D>L$. Since $1/\eps>Q/2$, we obtain
\[
 \frac{D^2}{\eps}>\frac{QL^2}{2}>\frac{n^2}{8}.
\]
The failed frontiers use $O(n^2)$ operations in total. The full dynamic
program under $w$ takes $O(n+n^2)$ time and space. The displayed
inequality places these costs within the claimed minimum bound up to a
constant factor.
Proposed and applied wording:
Suppose every call fails and let $L$ be the largest tested threshold.
At least one threshold is tested because $Q\le n^2$.
The next threshold $2L$ is excluded, so $QL^2=\Theta(n^2)$.
Failure at $L$ gives $D>L$. Since $Q=\Theta(1/\eps)$, we have
$D^2/\eps=\Omega(n^2)$.
The failed calls use $O(n^2)$ frontier operations and capped-cost queries
in total. The full dynamic program under $w$ takes $O(n+n^2)$ time
and space. These counts satisfy the claimed minimum bound after
including the logarithmic capped-query cost.
Reason: Keep the chain that pays for the fallback, remove irrelevant absolute constants, and distinguish capped queries from arithmetic time.

\[
 |\mu(A)-\mu(B)|

Apply this inequality to the fragments consumed by an alignment

The empty string is a reference point in the reverse triangle inequality, not the intermediate string in an upper-bound triangle inequality.
